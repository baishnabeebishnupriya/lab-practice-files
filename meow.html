The Caracal is a medium-sized wild cat famous for its striking black ear tufts
and incredible leaping agility.These elusive cats live in the dry savannas,
scrublands, and semi-deserts of Africa, the Middle East, and parts of India.
Their name comes from the Turkish word karakulak, which means "black ear."
Using specialized muscles in their large ears, caracals
can pinpoint the sounds of hidden prey. They are powerful jumpers
capable of springing over 10 feet into the air to knock birds down
in mid-flight. Primarily solitary and nocturnal, they survive on a diet of
small mammals, rodents, and birds
