Oleander

Oleander is a beautiful flowering plant with pink, white, or red flowers. However, all parts of the plant are poisonous if eaten. It contains toxic compounds that can affect the heart and cause serious illness. Therefore, oleander should be handled carefully and kept away from children and pets.
