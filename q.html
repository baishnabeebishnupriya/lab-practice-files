lists in html

Lists in HTML are structural elements used to group related
pieces of information in a clean, readable,

and organized manner.
Types of HTML Lists
Unordered Lists : Display items with bullet points where
the exact sequence does not matter.
Ordered Lists : Present items in a specific, numbered
sequence where order is important.
Description Lists : Pair specific terms
with their corresponding descriptions
or definitions, similar to a glossary.
